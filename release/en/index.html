Health and safety
Upholstery cleaning and dust mite treatment — why is it essential in your home?

An average sofa can harbour up to 2 million dust mites, whose droppings are among the most common indoor allergens. With our professional upholstery cleaning and dust mite treatment, we protect your family's health — using 100% chemical-free technology.

Dust mites: the largest source of indoor allergens in Hungary

Detectable numbers of dust mites live on upholstered furniture in 85% of Hungarian households. Their droppings are microscopic and, once airborne, cause sneezing, a runny nose, itchy eyes and asthma attacks. Regular upholstery cleaning is the most effective way to reduce dust mite allergens — we remove mites, their eggs and their droppings from even the deepest layers of fabric.

For families with young children: chemical-free upholstery cleaning

Babies' and young children's immune systems are still developing — they are particularly sensitive to indoor allergens and chemicals. Babies crawl on the sofa, suck on the upholstery and press their faces into pillows. ECO Clean upholstery cleaning is 100% chemical-free: we use no chlorine, ammonia or fragrances. After cleaning, the furniture is free from dust mites and bacteria, and completely safe even for the youngest children.

Pet owners: tackling pet hair, dander and dust mites together

Dog and cat hair, dander and saliva add another layer of allergens to upholstery — and dust mites feed on these very substances. The combined presence of pet hair and dust mites on furniture multiplies the allergen burden. Our professional upholstery cleaning removes pet hair, traces of shed skin cells and dust mites in one go — giving pets and families alike a healthier environment to live in.

Furniture hygiene for offices, accommodation and communal spaces

Used for 8 hours a day, swivel office chairs, meeting room furniture and hotel sofas are veritable breeding grounds for dust mites. Upholstered furniture in hospitality venues, clinics and educational institutions is used by dozens of people every day. Here, regular professional upholstery cleaning and dust mite treatment are a public health necessity, not a luxury. After our services, we provide a certificate of the dust mite treatment results on request.

Heating season and seasonal changes: the critical periods

The autumn and winter heating season in Hungary is particularly hazardous for allergy sufferers: closed windows, central heating and warm, dry air stir up dust mite particles and concentrate indoor allergens. In spring, the combined presence of pollen and dust mites makes symptoms worse. Upholstery cleaning at the change of seasons — ideally before the heating season and in spring — can prevent symptoms from worsening and keep your home healthier all year round.

What happens to the uploaded image?

When you select an image, only a local preview is created. With the “Start analysis” button you send the reduced photo and note via the ECO Clean server to an external image processing service, for photo-based material estimation. If you give separate consent, ECO Clean may also keep the prepared JPEG photo in private cloud storage and synchronise it to its own computer, for manual professional checking and classification. After professional approval, it may also pass the photo to an external image processing service as a comparison sample for later analyses. Without consent, no photo is added to the reference collection. We do not publish photos publicly. The photo should preferably show only the fabric or the care label.

ANDANTE furniture – Important information

Some ANDANTE furniture uses specialist fabrics, fabrics with fabric protection or water-sensitive fabrics that cannot be safely cleaned using hot water extraction for upholstery cleaning.

Please always check your furniture's fabric type and cleanability before ordering! Type ANDANTE furniture from Kanizsai Bútorgyár and similar "washable", water-repellent materials are typically not suitable for extraction (water-based) upholstery cleaning.

Consequences:

  • If we discover on arrival that the furniture's fabric cannot be cleaned using our technology, 50% of the service fee may be charged as a capacity reservation fee to cover the call-out fee and the lost booking slot.
  • If you are unsure about your furniture's fabric type, please check with our colleague in advance on 06 70 240 8141.

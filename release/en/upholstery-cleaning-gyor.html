The visible traces of everyday life

Why do headrests and armrests darken?

The natural oils of leather, sebum transferred to surfaces in contact with hair, cosmetic residues and household dust can all contribute to build-up. On the headrest, the armrest or the front of the seat where it touches the thighs, greyish, dark grey or even blackish discolouration may appear over time.

Between the fabric fibres

What the naked eye can't see.

House dust mites are microscopic organisms that can be found in mattresses, bedding and upholstered furniture. They feed on dead, shed human and animal skin cells. Their presence cannot be ruled out even in a carefully cleaned home.NIEHS: house dust mites and indoor allergens →

The allergens found in mite droppings and body fragments can trigger or worsen allergic complaints and asthmatic symptoms in sensitive people. House dust mites reproduce by laying eggs; a warm, humid environment can favour their development.University of Kentucky: house dust mite life cycle and allergens →

Regular cleaning serves to remove dust and dirt; complete freedom from mites, eggs and allergens cannot be promised. Humidity, bedding care and a suitable protective cover can all play a role in managing allergen load at home. With persistent symptoms, it is advisable to see an allergist.NIEHS: house dust mites and indoor allergens →

A few habits that make a big difference.

01

Gentle, regular dusting

Vacuum with a head suited to the fabric and on a setting approved by the manufacturer. Give attention to seams and crevices too; treat fresh liquid stains by careful blotting instead of hard rubbing.Kvadrat: cleaning and care of upholstery fabrics →

02

Bedding and humidity

Wash bedding weekly on a cycle allowed by the care label. Keeping relative humidity at around 40–50% can help create a less favourable environment for dust mites; you can track this with a hygrometer.AAAAI: professional guideline on reducing dust mite exposure →AAAAI: humidity and indoor allergy →

03

Enough time to dry completely

After cleaning, wait until the furniture is completely dry and ensure adequate air circulation. Do not place blankets or bedding on damp fabric; the time needed depends on the material and the environment.Muuto: materials and furniture care →

How often should upholstery or a mattress be cleaned?+

There is no one-size-fits-all interval for every home. Usage intensity, the presence of children and pets, visible soiling and the manufacturer's instructions together provide guidance. It is worth regularly checking heavily used surfaces.

Can a light-coloured or special-fabric sofa be cleaned?+

This can be judged from the material and the care instructions. Send a photo of the furniture and the label. Choosing the right method and testing in a hidden spot is especially important.Kvadrat: cleaning and care of upholstery fabrics →

Will every stain and discolouration disappear?+

Complete stain removal cannot be promised in every case. Old stains, home chemical treatment, colour loss or wear may limit the result. Tell us what happened to the surface and what you have tried to treat it with.

Is a dark armrest definitely dirt?+

It may be build-up, but wear, pile distortion or colour transfer can also affect the appearance. The difference matters because cleaning does not automatically restore damaged or faded fabric.

Does mattress cleaning eliminate allergies?+

We do not promise that. Treating allergies is a medical matter; reducing the allergen load at home may require several coordinated steps. Complete removal of dust mites cannot be guaranteed even in a clean home.NIEHS: house dust mites and indoor allergens →

Do dust mite eggs need attention too?+

Yes: eggs are part of the mites' life cycle. That is why it is worth thinking in terms of lasting care habits. A single clean cannot automatically be claimed to remove every mite and egg.University of Kentucky: life cycle and allergens of house dust mites →

When can I use the furniture again?+

After full drying. The time is also affected by the fabric, the filling, the temperature and air circulation. Do not cover the seat or mattress until then, and follow the drying instructions provided.Muuto: materials and furniture care →

What should I prepare for the consultation?+

The furniture type, quantity, approximate size, sides to be cleaned and the address. For a special stain or material, a close-up photo and a picture of the manufacturer's label are also useful.

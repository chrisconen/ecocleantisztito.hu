[Act CXII of 2011 on informational self-determination and freedom of information].

2. What data do we process, and why?

2.1. Online appointment booking (placing an order)

  • Data: your name, email address, telephone number, the address where the work will take place (postcode, town or village, street and house number), your selected items, extras and appointment, the estimated price, customer type (individual or business), your comments and whether you have accepted the condition relating to ANDANTE and water-sensitive furniture materials.
  • Purpose: recording and confirming your booking, arranging our arrival, carrying out the work and settling payment.
  • Legal basis: entering into and performing the contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). For data on accounting documents, a legal obligation (GDPR Article 6(1)(c), 2000. évi C. törvény 169. § [Section 169 of Act C of 2000 on accounting]).
  • How long: for 5 years after the work is completed, which is the limitation period for civil claims. We keep accounting documents for 8 years. If a booking does not lead to any work, we delete the data after 6 months.
  • Is it required? We cannot carry out the work without your name, email address, telephone number and address, so you cannot book without these details.

2.2. “Special circumstances” when booking, including allergies

  • Data: whether you tick the boxes indicating that there is a pet or young child in your home, or that “Someone in the family has an allergy”. The latter is health data, which is a special category of personal data.
  • Purpose: preparing for the work by recommending the appropriate treatment and preparing the work area.
  • Legal basis: performance of the contract for the pet and young child options (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). For the allergy option, your explicit consent (GDPR Article 9(2)(a)), which you give by ticking the box based on the information displayed next to it.
  • How long: we delete the allergy selection after completing the work. You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing info@ecocleantisztito.hu. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.
  • Is it required? No. You can book without ticking these boxes.

2.3. Quote requests for large orders

  • Data: your name or company name, email address, telephone number, the address where the work will take place, your comments, selected items and the estimated price.
  • Purpose: preparing a personalised quote and discussing the details with you.
  • Legal basis: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • How long: for 6 months from your quote request if it does not lead to an order. If it does, section 2.1 applies.

2.4. Business mattress cleaning quote requests (accommodation providers and businesses)

  • Data: the name of the accommodation provider or business, the contact person's name, email address and telephone number, the location (county, town or village), the number and type of mattresses, the time period and the message.
  • Purpose: preparing a quote and keeping in touch with the business.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in maintaining business relationships for the contact person's data (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)); for sole traders, steps before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • How long: for 1 year from the quote request. If it leads to an order, section 2.1 applies.

2.5. Photo-based material identification (ANDANTE screening)

  • Analysis: when you press the “Start analysis” button, we send the photo, resized by your browser, and your comments through our server to an external AI-based image processing provider to help identify the upholstery material. We do not retain the photo without your consent. The analysis is for guidance: we do not make automated decisions about you or carry out profiling.
  • Reference collection (optional): if you tick the separate box, we retain the photo in private cloud storage and may synchronise it to our own computer. A specialist manually checks and categorises it, and after approval we may share it with the image processing provider as a comparison sample. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). How long: until you withdraw your consent.
  • Expert review by email (optional): if you request this, we retain the photo, your comments and your email address so that we can reply. Legal basis: your consent. How long: for 1 year after our reply, or until you withdraw your consent.
  • Please send a photo showing only the furniture, with no people, documents or other personal data.

2.6. Telephone and email enquiries

  • Data: your telephone number or email address, your name and the content of your enquiry.
  • Purpose and legal basis: answering your question and providing a quote (GDPR Article 6(1)(b), or (f): our legitimate interest in communicating with customers).
  • How long: for 1 year after the matter is closed. If it leads to an order, section 2.1 applies.

2.7. Website operation and cookies

  • When you load the website, the hosting and content delivery providers (GitHub, Cloudflare) process your IP address and browser data for technical reasons (logging and preventing attacks). Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, functioning website (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).
  • We load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google.
  • We use Cloudflare Turnstile checks to prevent misuse: these use your browser's technical characteristics to determine whether a request comes from a person or an automated system.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising cookies. Only settings needed for the website to function remain in your browser's local storage (such as the items in your basket). These contain no personal data, and you can delete them in your browser at any time.
  • We display our Google reviews as publicly posted on Google (the review and the author's public name).

4. What rights do you have?

  • Access: you can ask which of your data we process and request a copy (GDPR Article 15).
  • Rectification: you can ask us to correct inaccurate data (Article 16).
  • Erasure: you can ask us to delete your data if it is no longer needed or if you withdraw your consent (Article 17). We cannot delete accounting documents because the law requires us to keep them.
  • Restriction: you can ask us to limit processing to storing your data for a period of time (Article 18).
  • Data portability: you can request your data processed on the basis of a contract or consent in a machine-readable format (Article 20).
  • Objection: you can object to processing based on legitimate interests (Article 21).
  • Withdrawal of consent: at any time, without giving a reason (Article 7(3)).

Send your request to info@ecocleantisztito.hu or to our registered office. We will reply without undue delay and within one month at the latest.

5. Remedies

If you feel we have infringed your rights, please write to us first so that we can resolve the issue. You can complain to the Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság [Hungarian data protection authority, NAIH] (1055 Budapest, Falk Miksa utca 9–11.; postal address: 1363 Budapest, Pf. 9.; email: ugyfelszolgalat@naih.hu; https://naih.hu), and you can also take the matter to court. You may also bring proceedings before the regional court for your permanent or temporary place of residence.

6. Data security

The website and booking system communicate exclusively through encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Only we have access to booking data, through password-protected accounts. In the event of a personal data breach, we act in accordance with the GDPR.

Why is mattress cleaning important?

1

Protecting health and reducing allergens

Mattresses provide an ideal habitat for dust mites, whose droppings are one of the most common indoor allergens. Dust mites can cause allergy symptoms, nasal congestion, coughing, eye irritation, asthma symptoms and skin problems. Annual dust mite treatment significantly reduces the allergen load, helping you feel better and sleep more healthily.

2

A cleaner, more hygienic sleeping environment

The average person sheds several hundred grams of dead skin cells each year, one of the main food sources for dust mites. Dust, bacteria and other contaminants can also build up in the mattress. Regular mattress cleaning removes these deposits, providing a fresher, cleaner and more hygienic environment for a good night's rest.

3

Extending the life of your mattress

With regular use, a mattress accumulates moisture, dirt and organic matter that gradually affect its condition. Professional cleaning and dust mite treatment help preserve its structure, comfort and hygiene, keeping it comfortable and usable for longer.

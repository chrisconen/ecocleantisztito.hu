How do we work?

WET upholstery cleaning: effective and gentle!

Hot water extraction upholstery cleaning is a wet cleaning process developed specifically to remove deep-seated dirt and stains from fabric upholstery. We use a high-pressure machine that injects a mixture of warm water and a special chemical-free cleaning agent deep into the fabric, then extracts the solution along with the dirt it contains.

Upholstery cleaning using hot water extraction is suitable only for fabric upholstery. We cannot effectively clean ANDANTE furniture from the Kanizsai furniture factory or similar ‘washable’, water-repellent materials using Kärcher extraction machines. Please ensure the area is ready for cleaning: remove nearby furniture that is sensitive to water spray; if the floor is water-sensitive (e.g. laminate flooring or parquet), cover it with a suitable material. We accept no liability for damage resulting from failure to meet these requirements!

⏰ When is upholstery cleaning recommended for sofas, armchairs and chairs?

Upholstery cleaning, just like washing our clothes, is recommended REGULARLY — a thorough upholstery clean once a year is a good idea. Dust mites, their eggs and their droppings must be removed from the foam-padded surfaces of sofas, armchairs, pouffes and chairs, followed by dust removal before machine upholstery cleaning. In hotels, schools, offices and other busy facilities, we recommend cleaning chairs and upholstered surfaces every six months, including dust mite treatment and sanitising.

🏠 DIY upholstery cleaning — what should you watch out for?

There are home remedies for upholstery cleaning too — you can read about dozens of miracle products online. BUT be careful! People often turn to us for help after cleaning their upholstery themselves: their freshly cleaned sofa is still stained, water rings appear, or the upholstery looks even greyer. Unfortunately, we cannot always help or repair the damage caused by incorrectly used miracle products.

🔧 Hiring an upholstery cleaning machine — is it worth it?

If you are considering upholstery cleaning, sooner or later you will come across a business happy to hire out an upholstery cleaning machine. When hiring out a machine, most do not ask what fabric your furniture is made from. Experienced upholstery cleaning professionals mix their own cleaning solutions to suit its condition, while dust mite treatment and dust removal are not even mentioned when hiring — suitable machines are not available for hire, although these steps are essential to the process. In short: you get what you pay for...

4. Cancellation and rescheduling

A booked appointment can be cancelled or amended free of charge no later than 48 hours before the call-out. For later cancellations, or where our colleagues cannot start work on site for reasons attributable to the customer, a capacity booking fee may be charged.

5. ANDANTE and water-sensitive materials

Extraction cleaning can only be used on fabric upholstery. ANDANTE type fabrics and other impregnated, “washable” or water-repellent materials cannot be safely cleaned with extraction technology. Please check your furniture's fabric type before ordering.

If it turns out on site that the furniture cannot be cleaned with the booked technology, 50% of the service fee, up to 30,000 HUF may be charged as a capacity booking fee to cover the reserved appointment and the wasted call-out. This condition must be accepted separately on the booking form.

6. Customer cooperation obligations

We cannot accept liability for damage resulting from moving furniture.

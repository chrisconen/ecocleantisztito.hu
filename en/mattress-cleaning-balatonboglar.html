Health effects

What can a dirty mattress cause?

Dust mite droppings are the most common indoor allergen – they can also cause serious health problems.

🤧

Allergy symptoms

Sneezing, a runny nose and nasal congestion at night and in the morning. Dust mite droppings are the most common indoor allergen and can worsen symptoms.

😴

Sleep disorders

Frequent waking, feeling tired in the morning, restless sleep. A dirty mattress reduces sleep quality, leading to daytime fatigue.

🫁

Worsening asthma

Dust mites are among the most common triggers of asthma attacks. They are particularly dangerous for children and older people.

👁️

Eye irritation

Itching, redness and watery eyes. Allergic conjunctivitis is often caused by dust mites living in the mattress.

🦠

Skin problems

Eczema, itchy skin and rashes. A dirty mattress can also harbour bacteria and fungi that cause skin problems.

😷

Headaches, feeling unwell

Chronic headaches, fatigue and difficulty concentrating. Allergic reactions can leave you feeling generally unwell.

⚠️ Regular cleaning is particularly important if:

  • A family member in your household has allergies or asthma
  • A young child or baby sleeps on the mattress
  • Pets also use the bed
  • You welcome guests as an accommodation provider
  • The mattress has not been cleaned for over 2 years

Healthy sleep

Why is mattress cleaning important?

1

Protecting health and reducing allergens

Mattresses provide an ideal habitat for dust mites, whose droppings are one of the most common indoor allergens. Dust mites can cause allergy symptoms, nasal congestion, coughing, eye irritation, asthma symptoms and skin problems. Annual dust mite treatment significantly reduces the allergen load, helping you feel better and sleep more healthily.

2

A cleaner, more hygienic sleeping environment

The average person sheds several hundred grams of dead skin cells each year, one of the main food sources for dust mites. Dust, bacteria and other contaminants can also build up in the mattress. Regular mattress cleaning removes these deposits, providing a fresher, cleaner and more hygienic environment for a good night's rest.

3

Extending the life of your mattress

With regular use, a mattress accumulates moisture, dirt and organic matter that gradually affect its condition. Professional cleaning and dust mite treatment help preserve its structure, comfort and hygiene, keeping it comfortable and usable for longer.

❓ When is mattress cleaning recommended?

For accommodation, we strongly recommend cleaning before and after the tourist season! For private homes, a refresh is worthwhile at least once a year, or every six months for people with allergies and families with young children.

❓ Is chemical-free cleaning safe?

Absolutely! We do not use harsh chemicals, so you can sleep on your mattress safely, straight after cleaning. No skin irritation, no chemical fumes – perfect for babies and allergy sufferers too.

❓ How long does a mattress take to dry?

Thanks to our industrial extraction technology, it is ready to use within 4-6 hours. In summer and heated homes, this can drop to 3-4 hours. In accommodation, a morning clean means it is ready to use by the evening!

❓ What types of mattresses do you clean?

Almost all types: sprung, foam, latex and memory foam mattresses. Due to the nature of our technology, we DO NOT clean waterbeds or specialist medical mattresses.

❓ Will the stains disappear?

Most stains (blood, urine, sweat, food) can be removed by 80-95%. For old, ingrained stains, we explain the expected results in advance so there are no surprises.

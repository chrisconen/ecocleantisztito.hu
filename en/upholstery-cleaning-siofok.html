How do we work?

WET upholstery cleaning: effective and gentle!

Hot water extraction upholstery cleaning is a wet cleaning process developed specifically to remove deep-seated dirt and stains from fabric upholstery. We use a high-pressure machine that injects a mixture of warm water and a special chemical-free cleaning agent deep into the fabric, then extracts the solution along with the dirt it contains.

Deep cleaning

Effectively removes stubborn stains, dirt and allergens even from the deepest layers of the fabric.

Freshness restored

Longer lifespan

Regular professional upholstery cleaning significantly extends the life of furniture and upholstery.

Frequently asked questions

Our Siófok customers' most frequently asked questions about upholstery cleaning

❓ What is the quickest way to make a booking?

You can currently arrange an appointment by calling 06 70 240 8141, but our AI-powered Online Booking System will soon be available, helping you find the most convenient time in seconds. During peak season, we recommend booking 1-2 weeks in advance.

❓ When is upholstery cleaning recommended in Siófok?

Highly recommended before and after the tourist season! For holiday homes and accommodation, clean before new guests arrive; for private homes, at least once a year. People with allergies and families with young children should freshen up their upholstery every six months. For wineries, we recommend cleaning before the season starts and after the grape harvest.

❓ Is chemical-free cleaning safe in food industry settings?

Absolutely! This is the cornerstone of our technology and one of its greatest advantages. We use no harsh chemicals, so you can safely use your furniture immediately after cleaning. No skin irritation, no chemical fumes – an ideal solution for restaurants and wine tastings.

❓ How long does upholstery take to dry?

Our industrial extraction technology keeps drying time to a minimum. Under normal conditions, it takes 4-6 hours, but in heated homes and during summer this can drop to 3-4 hours. At hospitality venues, furniture cleaned in the morning is ready to use by the afternoon.

❓ Do you take on larger jobs for wineries and hotels?

Yes, we specialise in serving business customers! We also offer annual maintenance contracts at discounted rates. Cleaning a typical tasting/reception area with 10-15 pieces of furniture takes half a day. For restaurants, we also offer overnight/early-morning work.

❓ What happens if I am not satisfied with the results?

We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you are unhappy with the results for any reason, we will return and repeat the work free of charge. In 15+ years, we have never had to honour this guarantee – but if we ever need to, we will do so without question.

❓ Which furniture is this cleaning method unsuitable for?

We do not clean leather-look upholstery (such as Andante types) or upholstery with specialist protective treatments, as our wet extraction method could damage these materials. For genuine leather furniture, we recommend a separate leather care service.

❓ What is the call-out fee for Siófok?

In central Siófok and nearby towns and villages (Badacsonytomaj, Badacsonytördemic, Badacsonyörs, Ábrahámhegy), the call-out fee is 3500 HUF in the town centre, 4000 HUF in the outskirts, 4500 HUF up to 10 km and 5500 HUF up to 20 km.

❓ Can I get a certificate for dust mite treatment?

Yes! On request, we issue an official dust mite treatment certificate confirming that the cleaning has been carried out and documenting the results. This is particularly useful for accommodation providers (Airbnb, Booking), nurseries and care homes, and may also be important for health reasons (e.g. as part of medical documentation for allergy sufferers).

❓ Payment methods?

We accept cash and card payments (on site) and bank transfers (in advance, or afterwards for business customers). Payment in instalments can also be arranged for larger jobs.

3. Services

On-site upholstery, mattress and carpet cleaning, dust mite and allergen treatment (extraction vacuuming with a HEPA filter and UV-C surface treatment), and the additional services listed on the website (such as stain removal and odour neutralisation). We carry out the work at the address you provide. Hiring an upholstery cleaning machine is subject to a separate hire agreement.

Cleaning reduces dust, skin flakes and dust mite allergens that have built up in upholstery and mattresses. It is not a medical treatment and cannot always remove changes caused by the furniture's age, material or previous treatment (fading, wear, burnt-in or old, ingrained stains, or dye transfer).

9. What you need to do

  • Provide parking near the property and a power supply for the equipment.
  • Make sure the furniture to be cleaned is freely accessible, and move valuables and items sensitive to water spray away from it. Let us know in advance if the flooring is sensitive to water (such as laminate flooring).
  • Keep pets away from the work area while we are working.
  • Arrange any moving of heavy furniture with us in advance.

10. Arrival and drying

We start the first appointment of the day at the agreed time. For other appointments, our arrival may vary by up to 30 minutes because of traffic or the previous job; we will let you know by telephone. Drying typically takes 4–24 hours, depending on the material, treatment and temperature. Only use the furniture once it is completely dry.
